Payment Risk Modernization Project

Project Overview

The Payment Risk Modernization project is designed to improve fraud detection
and transaction risk assessment for enterprise payment processing.

Fraud Model Ownership

Sarah Johnson is responsible for the fraud detection model.
The ML Engineering team owns model development and deployment.
The Risk Analytics team is responsible for model performance monitoring.

Transaction Approval Rules

Transactions with a risk score below 0.30 may be automatically approved.

Transactions with a risk score between 0.30 and 0.70 require additional
risk evaluation.

Transactions with a risk score above 0.70 must be reviewed by the Risk
Operations team before approval.

Compliance Requirements

All high-risk transactions must be retained for audit purposes.
Risk decisions must include the model version and decision timestamp.

Architecture

The risk platform uses an event-driven architecture. Transaction events
are published to Kafka and consumed by the risk scoring service.

The risk scoring service retrieves customer and transaction features,
generates a risk score, and stores the decision for downstream processing.
